# Storyboard: The Rock

- **Slug / Composition id:** `the-rock` / `TheRock`
- **Format:** Versus lanes (2 lanes)
- **Length:** 20s
- **Core message:** Consistency beats talent — the rock breaks from every hit, not the last one.
- **Metaphor:** a boulder = a big goal; each hammer hit = one day of work; cracks = invisible progress.
- **Cast:** TALENT — teal — neon — strong, flashy, impatient · CONSISTENCY — gold — neon — small, calm, relentless
- **Mood / palette:** `night` · teal vs gold (payoff gold light from inside the rock)
- **Music:** `pad-dark.wav` → payoff · calm → build (riser) → release (ding + boom)
- **Ending:** 2-line maxim

## Hook (first 1.5s)
Two lanes, two boulders, two figures holding hammers. "WHO BREAKS THE ROCK?" pops with a boom. DAY 0 · DAY 0.
