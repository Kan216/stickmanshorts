# Storyboard: The Climb

- **Slug / Composition id:** `the-climb` / `TheClimb`
- **Format:** Versus lanes (shared staircase) + counter
- **Length:** 15s
- **Core message:** Motivation starts fast and fades; habit is slow but reaches the top.
- **Metaphor:** a staircase = progress; each step lights gold when Habit takes it.
- **Cast:** MOTIVATION — teal — neon — fast, loud, fragile · HABIT — gold — neon — calm, steady, quiet
- **Mood / palette:** `night` · teal, gold (payoff)
- **Music:** `pad-hope.wav` · calm → build (riser) → release (ding)
- **Ending:** question to viewer

## Hook (first 1.5s)
Both figures at the foot of a dark staircase, "WHO REACHES THE TOP?" pops in with a boom. Counters 0/10.
